A once-weekly schedule is easy to forget because it doesn't happen every day. Pair your dose day with
something you already do each week, like a favorite show or a weekend walk.

## Set a reminder that fits you

Phone alarms, a paper calendar or a note on the fridge all work. Pick the one you'll actually see.

## Track it

Write down the date each week. If you ever wonder whether you took your dose, you'll know.
