Appointments go fast. Writing your questions down ahead of time helps you cover what matters.

## Good starting questions

- What should I expect in the first few weeks?
- Which side effects should I call you about?
- How will we know if treatment is working?

Try the [discussion guide](/treatment/discussion-guide.html) to build your own list.
